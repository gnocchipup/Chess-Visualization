Chess Visualization for Noobs — How to use

A browser app for drilling Lichess chess puzzles. Everything runs locally in your browser; an internet connection is required because each puzzle is fetched live from the Lichess API.

Create a puzzle set

  1. Download the puzzle database from database.lichess.org and decompress lichess_db_puzzle.csv.zst first (with zstd -d, 7-Zip, or similar). The app only accepts the plain, decompressed lichess_db_puzzle.csv.
  2. Click Puzzle sets in the app header.
  3. Choose the CSV file, set Rating min, Rating max, and Number of puzzles, give the set a name, and press Build set.

The set is saved in your browser (IndexedDB) and also offered as a downloadable .sqlite file, which you can re-import later with Import existing .sqlite. Keep sets to roughly 50,000 puzzles or fewer.

How to play

  1. Pick a set in the header dropdown and press New puzzle.
  2. The board shows the position a few moves before the puzzle starts; Ply back controls how much of that run-up is shown. The move table lists those context moves.
  3. Type your move in SAN (e.g. Nf3, Qxh7+) into the highlighted cell and press Enter. A missing or extra trailing +/# is fine.
  4. Or skip the typing: drag from one square to another (or tap one square, then the other) to enter the same move. Nothing moves on the board — the gesture is only solution input. Pawn promotions become queens unless the solution underpromotes.
  5. Correct → the opponent's reply is filled in automatically; continue with your next move. Incorrect → the move turns red and the puzzle is marked failed, but you can retry.
  6. On the final move, any legal move that delivers checkmate is accepted.
  7. Reveal solution fills in the remaining moves and marks the puzzle failed.
  8. The board flips automatically when you are solving as black; press F at any time to flip it manually.

The strip of squares under the header is your session score: green = solved with no wrong attempts, red = at least one wrong attempt.
